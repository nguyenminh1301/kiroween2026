## Text

The dialogue follows the Apollo 11 air to ground transcripts (NASA, public domain), through the Apollo in Real Time transcript data: https://apolloinrealtime.org/11/

The routine name `BURN_BABY_BURN--MASTER_IGNITION_ROUTINE` comes from the Luminary099 listing of the Apollo 11 lunar module program, transcribed by Virtual AGC and the MIT Museum (https://github.com/chrislgarry/Apollo-11). Public domain.

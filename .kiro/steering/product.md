# Product: LOSS OF SIGNAL

The site has two pages. `index.html` is a long, plain article about the flight of Apollo 11 (real names, NASA photos, sources, no game code). `play.html` is the game: a first-person, PS1-style horror game for desktop browsers, set on the Apollo 11 flight. Kiroween 2026 entry, Costume Contest category: the interface must feel haunted and polished, and every spooky element must serve how the game plays. Both "Play as an astronaut" buttons in the article open `play.html`. A run takes about 12 minutes over six chapters. A chapter card (title and date over black) bridges each one.

## Pages and menus
- The article ends with the section "What really happened" and "Sources". It shows "Seen in your flight" or "Missed in your flight" on each of the eight real moments if this browser has played a run.
- The game has no title menu. `play.html` shows one card, "Use headphones. Click to begin.", and one click starts act 0 (and unlocks sound and pointer lock).
- The Esc menu holds Resume, Restart checkpoint, Chapters (same unlock rule), Settings (sensitivity, volume, subtitle size, reduced motion) and Back to the article.
- When act 5 ends, the picture fades to black and the article opens at "What really happened". There is no in-game Archive screen.

## Cast
Player is Buzz Aldren, Lunar Module Pilot. Neil Armstead (commander, CDR). Mike Collier (command module pilot, CMP). Gunter Went leads the pad crew. Houston is CAPCOM. Faces never show: helmets on, or visors down in close shots.

## Acts
| Act id | Title | Beats |
|---|---|---|
| `act0-pad` | Pad 39A | Dawn on the swing arm. Narrator, two voiced lines. Wave at the crowd far below and they cheer. Walk to the White Room, press E on the hatch: the handle turns, the hatch swings open onto a closed, dark cabin box, fade, and act 1 starts with Buzz in the seat. |
| `act1-launch` | Launch | About 10 s in the cabin from ignition to orbit, most beats on outside shots. Strapped in with the eye 0.7 m above the seat and the main panel and DSKY in front, bright cabin with checklists, cue cards, a pencil on a string and a camera. Ignition, liftoff, tower cleared, roll program, max Q, staging, launch escape tower and cover jettison (windows uncovered), second and third stage, orbit, cuts between cabin and Saturn V outside shots. Then TLI: a card, three DSKY keys, the burn, Earth in the window. The shake stays under 0.4 degrees and 1 cm. |
| `act2-coast` | Coast | Zero gravity, the spacecraft turns once every 120 s. Float to the lander by grabbing rails and pushing off. Three tasks: the oxygen line (three taps), the pressure gauge, and the computer (three red lines to click until green). A chain of handrails reaches every place, up to the sleeping bags. The tunnel is longer, with Mike's line. Hold C to close the eyes in the sleeping bag: fade to black and the act ends. |
| `act3-farside` | Far side | Buzz's dream, played as real until he wakes. He starts in his sleeping bag, the other two bags empty, and walks (Earth gravity) a rust-red map lit by flashlight: a junction with a window onto the lit room where the crew hangs, a jammed hatch with light under it, a left and a right loop with side rooms, lockers and empty bags to hide in (no door corners, wide slits in the bags), and a hall in front of the cage room. The creature hunts the last place it heard a noise, searches, then patrols. Noise meter rises with movement and falls when you hold your breath (SPACE). A voice like Houston calls, though Houston cannot reach them. Neil and Mike hang from the ceiling of the cage room. Open it, carry one back to the bags (slower, louder), then go back for the other. The cage door glows and a marker shows where to carry them. The creature fits the corridor. When Buzz takes him, the creature swallows the view in about 1.5 s: lit face, split head, teeth closing, black. Buzz wakes in the real, bright cabin, the crew turned toward him, and picks one of two answers. Checkpoints: `start`, `wp0`, `wp1`, `wp2`, `wp3`. Caught: the swallow, then restart from the last checkpoint after 1 s of black. |
| `act4-moon` | Tranquility | Powered descent with cuts between outside shots and two cabin shots (panel and window, helmet height, standing). The real 1202 alarm, reaction control puffs, plume, dust sheet, "contact light", "The Eagle has landed". Neil climbs down first, seen from the hatch, then Buzz climbs down (nine rungs, a one metre drop to the footpad). Mike stays in orbit and speaks on the radio. Low gravity, jumping works. Three tasks in any order: the US flag (stripes, stars, rod on top), soil sample, seismometer. The photo comes first: the soil sample and the seismometer stay locked until it is taken. After the flag, with the player inside the mark and looking at the flag, E starts the photo: Neil stands a few metres away, lifts the camera, flash and shutter click, then the real NASA photo AS11-40-5875 shows as a print. Neil then works near the lander (scoops soil, kneels at rocks, writes on his cuff checklist, takes photos). Then back to the ladder and up. The Moon is calm: the radio and the player's breathing only. At liftoff the ascent stage separates, a long narrow flame fires down onto the descent stage, the flag falls, reaction control puffs fire, and the routine name `BURN_BABY_BURN--MASTER_IGNITION_ROUTINE` shows on a green AGC readout panel. The next act loads before the shot ends and the picture fades to black. Silent surface except radio (no air). Checkpoints: `start`, `tasks`, `run` (back at the ladder after the tasks). |
| `act5-home` | Splashdown | Seated at helmet height. Re-entry shake at the launch cap, strongest at peak heating and the chute openings. Blackout, cover, two 5 m drogues (reefed, then open), three 25 m mains in a triangle seen from below, each on its own line to the capsule, release after splashdown, Pacific splashdown with a soft sea under the voices. USS Hornet, a destroyer, Sea King helicopter NAVY 66. Happy ending: a narrator line, then the Archive. |

## Archive
Lives in the article as the section "What really happened", read after the game ends. It lists the real Apollo 11 moments the game shows, each with what the player saw, the real fact and a source, marked seen or missed (only when this browser has played): the launch escape tower and its cover leaving, the loss of signal behind the Moon, the 1202 alarm, "The Eagle has landed", the seismometer picking up footsteps, the flag knocked over by the ascent engine, `BURN_BABY_BURN--MASTER_IGNITION_ROUTINE`, USS Hornet and Helicopter 66. It keeps the NASA photo credit. It does not show the act 3 answer line. Nothing in it hints at the creature or at a fourth person.

## Controls
Mouse look with pointer lock, WASD move, E use, SPACE hold breath (act 3) or jump (act 4), C close eyes (act 2), Q look back, F check wrist, Esc pause menu.

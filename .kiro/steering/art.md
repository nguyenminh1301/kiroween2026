# Art

PS1 look: low-poly geometry, low-res nearest-filtered textures, vertex snapping, ordered dither, fog, low internal resolution scaled up. Textures are generated in code on canvas and cached. All geometry is built in code, no external models. Target look: `docs/reference/shots/` (see its README). The green stats box in them is a debug overlay.

## Period UI
- Objective: a typed line on a paper checklist strip at the top of the screen, key in a small box.
- Dialogue: a box with the speaker's name.
- Menus look typed.
- Fonts: Jost and IBM Plex Mono (OFL).
- No HUD except the center dot, a verb label when something can be used, and the act 3 noise meter.
- No NASA insignia or logos.
- The creature is never fully lit: a silhouette, partly seen, at a distance or at the edge of the flashlight.

# Requirements: playtest 3, acts 4 and 5

Dialogue is unchanged.

1. Flag photo
   1.1 The photo mark SHALL sit about 1.5 m farther from the planted flag than before, still beside it.
   1.2 WHEN the player stands inside the mark, looks at the flag and presses E, the system SHALL start the photo.
   1.3 The mark SHALL stay visible and the hint SHALL say to stand in it, look at the flag and press E.
   1.4 WHILE the photo is not taken, the soil sample and the seismometer SHALL be locked, and using them SHALL show a hint.
2. Re-entry outside shot
   2.1 The command module SHALL fly heat shield first, tilted about 20 degrees off its path.
   2.2 A thin additive shell SHALL hug the shield: white and yellow at the centre, orange to red at the rim.
   2.3 A long wake SHALL stream back many capsule lengths, fading orange to dim red, with sparks and flakes falling behind.
   2.4 The camera SHALL follow from behind and aside, above a curved dark blue Earth with a thin dawn line. The capsule SHALL shake slightly.
   2.5 The glow SHALL build, peak and fade over the shot.
3. Parachutes
   3.1 The two drogues SHALL show reefed, then fully open as round ribbon cones, each on its own riser to the capsule top.
   3.2 The three mains SHALL form a triangle seen from below, canopies tilted outward.
   3.3 Each main SHALL have its lines gather to its own point above the capsule and its own riser to the capsule top. No shared line.
   3.4 Lines and risers SHALL be thick enough to see in outside shots.
   3.5 Deploy order, group sway and release after splashdown SHALL stay.

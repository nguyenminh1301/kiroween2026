# Requirements: playtest 2, the act 3 dream

Story is unchanged: Buzz wakes in the dream, the crew is missing, he finds them at the far end, carries one back, goes back for the other, and the creature swallows him. Then he wakes in the real cabin.

## 1. A map with choices
1.1 The direct way from the junction to the cage room shall be blocked by a jammed hatch with light under it.
1.2 The map shall offer a left loop and a right loop. Each loop shall have side rooms and dead ends. Both loops shall meet in a hall in front of the cage room.
1.3 The rust corridor art, floor strips and red ambient shall stay.
1.4 The checkpoints shall be `start` at the bags, `wp0` where the loops split, `wp1` at the cage, `wp2` at the bags with the first crewman, `wp3` back at the cage.

## 2. More places to hide
2.1 Both loops shall have lockers, and one more locker shall stand close to the cage room.
2.2 When the player steps behind an open door into its dark corner and stands still, the creature shall not see them.
2.3 When the player presses E at an empty sleeping bag on a crew bay wall, the view shall go dark with a thin slit of light, and the creature shall not see them.
2.4 In every hiding spot the creature shall not see a player who stays still and quiet.

## 3. An easier creature
3.1 The creature shall hunt the last place it heard a noise, not the player.
3.2 When it reaches that place and finds nothing, it shall search around for a few seconds, then wander off on a patrol route.
3.3 Leaving a hiding spot shall make a small noise and shall not tell the creature where the player is.
3.4 The noise threshold shall be higher than before, and the noise meter shall fall faster when the player stands still.
3.5 The creature shall walk a path of waypoints through door and corner centres and shall never cut across a wall corner.
3.6 The creature shall never enter a locker, a door corner or a bag. It may stop outside, breathe and leave.
3.7 The creature shall face the way it walks, with a forward walk cycle.
3.8 While it hunts, the creature shall make one quiet click, scrape or breath every 8 to 15 seconds from its position. Loud steps and the scream shall play only during a chase. No creature sound shall loop.

## 4. The captured crew
4.1 Neil and Mike shall hang upside down by the ankles from the ceiling of the cage room, arms dangling, turning slowly.
4.2 Dark blood smears shall mark the walls and floor below them, with a few slow drips.
4.3 Helmets shall stay on with visors up and no face shown.
4.4 They shall whisper the lines in item 7. E on one shall lower him onto Buzz's shoulders as before.

## 5. The swallow
5.1 The swallow shall play at the end (touching the second crewman) and whenever the creature catches the player.
5.2 The swallow shall run: creature in front of the camera 0.25 s, head splits open with teeth filling the view 0.35 s, a hard camera jolt and the scream, then jaws slam shut and cut to black with no fade. Total under 1 s.
5.3 When caught, the act shall restart at the last checkpoint after 1 s of black. At the end, the black shall lead to waking up.

## 6. Waking in a bright cabin
6.1 The wake scene shall use the act 2 cabin with its normal lights on, the front of the command module and panels present.
6.2 Neil and Mike shall lie in their sleeping bags, looking normal, turned toward Buzz.
6.3 The choice prompt shall show both options at the bottom, readable. The pointer shall be free while it shows. Keys 1 and 2 and a mouse click shall pick, and the hover state shall be clear.

## 7. Dialogue
7.1 The act 3 lines shall be replaced with the text in the prompt. All are subtitles only, with no recording.

3. Hatch. WHEN the player presses E on the act 0 hatch, THE game SHALL turn the handle, swing the hatch open with a creak and a hiss, fade, and load act 1 with the player in the seat. The boot print anomaly and line SHALL be gone.
4. Launch. THE act 1 launch SHALL last about 150 s of game time with these beats: ignition and liftoff, tower cleared, roll program, max Q, staging, tower jettison, second stage, third stage, orbit shutdown. Then TLI as before (card, three DSKY keys, burn, Earth). There SHALL be no oxygen check, red switch or `o2-for-four` anomaly. The shake cap stays.

6. Earth. THE spacecraft SHALL turn slowly, one turn every 120 s, so the Earth and stars drift across the windows. Only the Earth window SHALL show the Earth.
7. Repairs. Act 2 SHALL keep the oxygen line and the pressure gauge, and add a six switch breaker panel with a card, and a landing site keypad (five digits, ENTR, OPR ERR on a wrong number). WHEN a close-up task ends, or the player presses Esc or moves away, THE player SHALL move and use E again.
8. Bedtime. THE act SHALL NOT play breathing rhythms. Holding C SHALL fade to black in 2 s and the act SHALL end a few quiet seconds later. `fourth-sleeper` and `tunnel-longer` SHALL be removed. The tunnel stays longer, with Mike's line.
